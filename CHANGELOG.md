# Changelog — Mochi Pop!

## [0.5.8] - 2026-11-15
### Improved
- **Cozy Room & 3D Toys Overhaul:** Added wall baseboard trim, wooden plank floor texture, 4-pane window mullions, stitched double-rim rugs, clearer wall picture framing, and richly shaded 3D room gadgets and toys (ceramic bowl with golden fish, wool yarn ball, wind-up mouse, feather wand, laser pointer beam, flower water fountain, kitty auto-feeder, catnip plant, cat tower, cat wheel, and animated songbird).
- **3D Candy Blocks & Vector Special Tiles:** Upgraded 8×8 recessed checkerboard cells, inlaid board housing, golden row/column line-clear preview highlights, glossy candy/jelly blocks, faceted crystal ice, and crisp custom vector emblems for `Fish`, `Bomb`, `Gift`, `Rocket`, `Rainbow`, and `Tuna`.
- **Tablet & Mobile Layout Polish:** Centered Mochi neatly on her rug on tablet screens, scaled tablet room visuals proportionally, separated speech bubbles and event banners from covering the room window, and added depth & glossy highlights across HUD pills, meters, tray slots, action buttons, and Closet cards.

## [0.5.7] - 2026-11-15
### Improved
- **Clean Default Look:** Mochi starts completely bare with no accessories or collars equipped by default (`Red Bell` remains unlocked for free in the Closet).
- **Upgraded 3D Wardrobe Accessories:** Redesigned all 9 head accessories, knitted scarves, and 3 bell collars with layered 3D gradients, shadows, gemstones, glossy lenses, and cushioned details.
- **Intuitive Closet Button:** Updated the top-right Closet button to `🛍️` for instant recognition.

## [0.5.6] - 2026-11-15
### Fixed & Improved
- **Smooth Motion-Tracked Accessories & Collars:** Hats, crowns, bows, headphones, sunglasses, scarves, and bell collars smoothly follow Mochi's head tilt, facing direction, and body motion across every animation loop.

## [0.5.5] - 2026-11-14
### Added & Improved
- **UI & Touch Ergonomics:** Compact narrow-phone HUD, live `✨ 1 LINE!` / `🔥 2 LINES!` / `💣 3×3` placement clear badge, configurable **Touch Drag Height** (`Low / Normal / High`), and **Tablet Quick-Care Dock** (`💕 / 🧶 / 🎩 / 🎨`).
- **Seasonal Window Weather & Winter Cozy Buff:** Animated seasonal weather outside Mochi's window plus extra mood warmth during Winter when the Heater gadget is active.
- **Ad-Free Daily Archive Option & Special Block Tips:** Watch a rewarded ad or spend Fish to replay missed days, plus 1-time localized tips when encountering special blocks.
- **Offline PWA & Local File Backup:** Export/import `.json` progress backups inside Cloud & Backup.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-14
### Added & Improved
- **Monthly Daily Archive:** Browse the full monthly calendar, replay missed seeded challenges, and unlock `7d / 15d / Full Month` milestone Fish rewards.
- **Weekly Purr League Season Rewards:** Claim end-of-season Fish prizes and Season Trophies for top weekly ranks.
- **Colorblind Symbols & 30 FPS Power Saver:** Distinct geometric block symbols plus optional calm low-battery frame pacing across all 6 languages.

## [0.4.6] - 2026-11-14

- **Landscape Tablet Layout & Ambient Audio:** Split-screen landscape mode for tablets/foldables plus dynamic night cricket ambience and automatic in-app update checks.
